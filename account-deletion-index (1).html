Account Deletion

TCL TV Remote - Smart Control
Developer: Veevan Studioz
You can request permanent deletion of your app account and associated account data from this page.

How to request deletion

Use the email address associated with your app account, if possible, and tap the button below. A pre-filled email will open. Send the request to Veevan Studioz.

DELETE MY ACCOUNT

Contact email: veevanstudioz@gmail.com

What will be deleted?

  • Your Firebase Authentication account.
  • Your account email and Firebase UID held in the app's account records.
  • Your associated Firestore account record, including Premium/Founder entitlement information.
Important: If your account has Founder Lifetime Premium or another account-based Premium entitlement, deleting the account will remove that entitlement. It may not be recoverable after deletion.

Data stored on your device

TV connection settings and other locally stored app preferences can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Data retention

After a valid deletion request is completed, we do not intend to retain deleted account data except where limited information must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory reasons. Any such retention will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable law.

In-app option

Signed-in users can also open the Premium/Account area in the app and select Delete Account to start an account deletion request.
